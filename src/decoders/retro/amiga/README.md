# Amiga audio

Candidates: 8SVX, 16SV, MAUD, SoundTracker MOD, MED, OKT, AHX, HivelyTracker and other well-documented replay formats. Share IFF parsing with `../../containers/`. Tracker modules and synthetic music are not ordinary PCM files and should use a separate replay interface.

`maktabasauti_svx.c` decodes IFF 8SVX. `maktabasauti_mod.c` is an original replay for the MOD family: 31-instrument signatures (`M.K.`, `M&K!`, `M!K!`, `FLT4`, `xCHN`, `xxCH`, `xxCN`, `TDZ1`–`TDZ3`, `CD81`, `OCTA`, `OKTA`), StarTrekker `FLT8` as a pair of 4-channel patterns, and the signature-less 15-instrument header. The replay mixes Paula periods. Stereo pan repeats the Amiga pairs, left right right left. `Cxx`, `Fxx`, arpeggio, portamento, vibrato, tremolo, volume slide, sample offset, panning, `Bxx`, `Dxx` and `Exx` except `E0x` and `EFx` change playback. A jump back onto a row already played ends the song. `E6x` repeats a section a finite number of times and does not trip that end. Finetune does not change the written period; arpeggio and glissando use its column. Waveform 3 stays a sine. After the last order, a restart byte other than `0x78` or `0x7F` and below the song length continues at that order when its first row has not started. A jump back onto a row already played still ends the song. This decoder is not registered in the player. The mixer is C until the render is measured; it is not assembly yet.

No other Amiga format is claimed.
