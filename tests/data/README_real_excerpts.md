# Real xHE-AAC excerpts (usac_r1 .. usac_r4.m4a)

Short excerpts (whole access units, cut at random access points) of the public xHE-AAC demo files of Fraunhofer IIS
(<https://www2.iis.fraunhofer.de/AAC/xhe-aac-abr.html>, "Sintel" at 24 and 64 kbit/s).

* The audio is from the film *Sintel* (c) Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org, licensed under
  [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).  Attribution: "Sintel, (c) copyright Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org".
* The encoded streams are the demo content published by Fraunhofer IIS; the excerpts are unmodified access units.
* They are the only streams of a production encoder in the repository.  What each one pins: `make_usac_real_excerpts.sh`.
